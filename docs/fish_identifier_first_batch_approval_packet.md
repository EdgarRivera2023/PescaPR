## Approver instructions and independence rule

For each candidate, Edgar Rivera should independently verify that:

1. the linked source item corresponds to the candidate;
2. the displayed fish reasonably matches the proposed species;
3. the displayed license/evidence is visible and consistent with this packet;
4. no obvious contradictory copyright or deletion warning appears; and
5. no obvious quality, privacy, source-correlation, or other exclusion reason is present.

The Dataset Approver is not being asked to redo specialist taxonomy work. This packet contains only LOW *Coryphaena* and clear adult MEDIUM *Alectis*. HIGH/VERY_HIGH specialist review remains a separate gate and those species are excluded.

For CC BY items, approval confirms that PescaPR is prepared to preserve creator, source, license link, and any required change notice in dataset records. Public-domain status does not remove the need to preserve provenance and requested courtesy credit.
